>_ ABOUT

1313HEART™ specializes in retro clothing, publications & objects. With a focus on music, the arts and counter culture. 1313HEART™ is dedicated to pushing the transition to sustainable consumption through curation, sourcing and reference points.

>_ What's "One Credit"?

"One Credit" originates from arcade culture, meaning one coin, one chance.
There is no restart, no continue — every decision must be made within this single attempt.

Beginning in 2026, 1313heart is launching a new series of projects, intermittently integrating the concept of “one credit” across various themes, conveying our understanding and messages regarding creation, style, and contemporary observations.
