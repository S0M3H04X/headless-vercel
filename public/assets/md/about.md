## About

1313HEART™ specializes in used clothing, publications & objects. With a focus on music, the arts and counter culture. 1313HEART™ is dedicated to pushing the transition to sustainable consumption through curation, sourcing and reference points.

## Shipping

Most of the time orders are fulfilled next day, tracking will be provided via email. Please see below for estimated delievery times.

UK: 2/3 days  
Europe: 4/5 days  
USA: 5/7 days  
Rest of World: 7/10 days

For high value orders (around £400+) a courier / expidited service will be used and delivery times will be substantially quicker.
